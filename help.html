Frequently Asked Questions

How can I enroll in a course?
Open the Courses section from your dashboard and select the course you want to learn.
How can I attempt a quiz?
Open your enrolled course, select the required module and click on the quiz option.
How can I check my progress?
Go to the My Progress section from your dashboard to view your learning progress.
When can I get my certificate?
Your certificate becomes available after completing all required course modules and assessments.
What should I do if a course is not loading?
Refresh the page and check your internet connection. If the problem continues, report the issue using the form below.
